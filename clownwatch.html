🚔 Police dispatch feed — coming soon

Automated scanner-audio monitoring isn't in v1 on purpose: Broadcastify's terms prohibit programmatic access, transcription, and alerting on their feeds without a commercial license, and most dispatch audio is moving to encrypted channels anyway. The honest v2 paths are: (1) polling police departments' public calls-for-service / blotter feeds, (2) incident aggregators like SpotCrime, or (3) crowd-sourced tips from scanner listeners — which starts below.
